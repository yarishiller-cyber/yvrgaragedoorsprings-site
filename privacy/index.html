Privacy policy.

Plain language. We collect the minimum we need to send a tech to your driveway. That's it.

What we collect

When you call us: your phone number (via caller ID), your address (when you tell us), and a description of what broke. We use this to dispatch a tech and quote the job. We store the call record for service-history purposes and to follow up on warranty work.

When you text us: your phone number, the photo or message you sent, and our reply. Same dispatch and warranty purposes.

When you email us: your email address and the content of the message.

When you submit the quote form on this site: postal code, phone number, and the short description of the issue. The form sends this to info@yvrgaragedoorsprings.ca via your email client (mailto). We do not store form submissions on a third-party server.

When you visit this website: standard server logs (IP address, referring URL, browser type, timestamp) for security and basic traffic counting. We use your approximate location, derived from your IP address, to show service availability for your area on the homepage. We do not store this location data.

What we don't do

  • We do not sell or share your phone number, email, or address with third parties.
  • We do not put you on a marketing list.
  • We do not use behavioural retargeting ads.
  • We do not have a chat widget collecting transcripts.
  • We do not require cookies for personalization (the geolocation lookup is client-side and not stored).

Third-party services we use

  • ipapi.co — for the approximate-location lookup on the homepage. Your IP address is sent to ipapi.co; we don't store the response. ipapi.co privacy policy.
  • Hostinger — our website host. They keep standard server logs per their privacy policy.
  • Google Search Console / Bing Webmaster Tools — aggregated traffic and ranking data. No personally identifying info.

If we add Google Ads tracking or call-tracking software (CallRail, for example) in the future, we'll update this page first.

Cookies

This site sets no first-party tracking cookies. Third-party services we use may set their own — those are governed by their respective privacy policies (linked above).

Your rights under PIPEDA

Under Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA), you have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Request corrections to inaccurate information.
  • Withdraw consent to ongoing use of your information.
  • File a complaint with the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

To exercise any of these rights, email info@yvrgaragedoorsprings.ca. We respond within 30 days.

Children

We don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you're under 18, have a parent or guardian make the call.

Changes

If we update this policy, we update the date at the top. Material changes get noted here.
