The model

Sixteen cities. One tech in each. Roughly 12 minutes from your door.

Most garage door companies in Greater Vancouver work from one or two dispatch points and charge you (sometimes hidden in the quote) for the drive across the bridges. We don't. The technician who shows up at your house lives in your city. Surrey's tech lives in Surrey. Tsawwassen's tech lives in Tsawwassen. The wrapped van is parked somewhere near you when you make the call.

That's the whole pitch. There's no proprietary software, no "service circle membership," no after-hours upcharge. The price you hear on the phone is the price on the invoice. The door doesn't lie. Neither do we.

What we don't do

We don't sell doors. We don't sell openers. We sell one thing.

If your door is rotting at the bottom panel, your seal is dry-rotted, or your opener gear is grinding — we'll tell you. We're not the right shop to fix those. We'll refer you to a tech we trust and step out.

What we do is replace residential torsion and extension springs, and the cables that work with them. Same-day. Flat-rate. Same price 7 a.m. or 9 p.m. Tuesday or Saturday. That's it.

It's a small surface area. That's the point.
