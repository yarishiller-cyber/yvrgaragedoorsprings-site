Your Richmond tech

Knows the river-air corrosion problem cold.

Richmond is the salt-air capital of the Lower Mainland. The city sits at or below sea level on the Fraser estuary, with humidity that doesn't quit and salt that comes off the Strait of Georgia like clockwork. Galvanized springs and cables don't survive here. If yours snapped early — under 7 years — corrosion was probably the cause, not the cycle count.

We swap to oil-tempered standard or stainless springs depending on how close you are to open water. Cables get the same treatment — and they're free with the two-spring tier, which matters in Richmond more than anywhere else because cables fail first when the air is wet and salty.

The housing here splits roughly two ways: 1970s–80s ranchers on flat lots (still common in Broadmoor, Seafair, Hamilton) and 2000s–2020s monster-home rebuilds with 3-car garages and oversized double doors that need 2⅝" ID, .250+ wire gauge springs. Your tech stocks both. If you live in Steveston, we work in close partnership with Steveston Garage Doors — same family-feel team for the historic neighbourhood.

Richmond-specific questions

The ones we get from this city.

My spring rusted out at year 4. Is that normal in Richmond?

Sadly, yes — if your previous installer used galvanized springs and standard cables. Salt air on a flat coastal city eats them. We use oil-tempered or stainless on every Richmond install, and cables are included free with the two-spring tier.

I have an oversized double door on a monster home in Broadmoor. Special spring size?

Usually 2⅝" inside diameter, .250+ wire gauge, 36" or longer. We stock these — your tech will measure and confirm, but we're not driving back to a warehouse.

I'm in Steveston — are you the same crew as Steveston Garage Doors?

Sister company. We coordinate with them directly for Steveston (Lulu Island) work. Same family voice on the phone, same standards. Either number gets you the same crew.

Hamilton/Queensborough — do you cross over from Burnaby fast?

Hamilton sits right by the Queensborough Bridge. From our Still Creek HQ that's roughly 12 minutes, no Massey Tunnel.
