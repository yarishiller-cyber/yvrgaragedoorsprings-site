What we look for

The basics that earn a referral.

  • A registered business in BC with a real service history.
  • Liability insurance and WorkSafeBC coverage (or a clear plan to get there).
  • Quality workmanship and customers who'd vouch for you.
  • Fair, transparent pricing — quoted before the work, no surprises at the door.
  • Coverage in one or more of our sixteen Greater Vancouver cities.
