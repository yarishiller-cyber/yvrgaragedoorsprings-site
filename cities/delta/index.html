Your Delta tech

Two cities in one — and Tsawwassen has its own page.

Delta splits cleanly: North Delta (Sunshine Hills, Nordel) sits up on the bluff, drier, slightly elevated. Ladner is on the Fraser delta, at or below sea level, with humidity and salt that match Richmond. Your Delta tech is set up for both — oil-tempered standard for North Delta, oil-tempered or stainless for Ladner depending on proximity to open water.

The housing in both halves is mostly 1970s–80s split-levels and ranchers, which means a huge volume of original-spec springs at or past their 10,000-cycle life. If your spring just snapped and your house was built between 1975 and 1985, you got 40 years out of it. That's the math — and that's why we're getting the call.

Ladner has a fair amount of agricultural-edge homes with detached shops running commercial-style doors (10' or 12' wide, heavier springs). We handle those too — different spec but same flat-rate logic per door.

If you're in Tsawwassen specifically, see the Tsawwassen page — different salt-air profile, different housing mix, different tech.

Delta questions

The ones we get from this city.

I live in Ladner. Will the salt do to me what it does in Richmond?

Similar — slightly less because Ladner has more inland buffer. We use oil-tempered or stainless springs depending on proximity to open water. Cables free with the two-spring tier.

My North Delta home is 1980s with original springs. Worth doing both?

Almost always yes. Same install date = same fatigue. Doing both for $48 more saves the second call-out 3–8 months out.

I have a 12-foot wide barn-style shop door in rural Ladner. Quote?

Yes — heavier wire-gauge springs at parts upcharge. We'd measure and quote you on site before winding.

How fast is "15 minutes" given the Alex Fraser?

From our Burnaby HQ via Alex Fraser, North Delta is ~25 min — but your North Delta tech doesn't start in Burnaby. They start in Delta. So 12–18 minutes from when you call to when they're at your door.
